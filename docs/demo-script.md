# Demo video script (target: 2:00–2:30)

Record at 1440×900 or 1920×1080, browser zoom 100%, light mode, with the cursor highlight on.
Before recording, open the site once and run the demo, so the Bedrock calls are warm.

| Time | Screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Landing hero | "Kids now talk to AI like a best friend. ChatGPT moderates for adults, but it doesn't know it's talking to a 14-year-old, and it only ever sees one chat at a time. Nightlight sees the pattern." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Scroll to "What ChatGPT won't flag" cards | "A diet plan, instructions for hiding an app, a gift card from an older 'friend' on Roblox. Each one is fine on its own for an adult, and a warning sign for a child." |
| 0:30–0:45 | Upload card → export tabs → click **Try the demo** | "A parent drops in the Chrome history file and a ChatGPT export. Everything is parsed in the browser, and addresses and school names are redacted before anything is sent." |
| 0:45–1:00 | Analysing screen (counters, batches) | "Claude analyses the redacted data against our Child Safety Lens, a 14-dimension rubric built on the 4Cs of online risk, the APA's 2025 advisory on AI and adolescents, and UNICEF's guidance, calibrated to the child's age." |
| 1:00–1:20 | Overview: status, KPIs, top concerns, positives | "The parent gets a calm summary, not a raw log: what's going well, and what's worth a conversation this week." |
| 1:20–1:40 | Concerns tab → open one card → **Reveal** evidence → copy the conversation starter | "Each concern explains why it matters for this age, why the chatbot didn't flag it, and gives a warm way to start the conversation. Evidence stays blurred unless the parent chooses to look." |
| 1:40–1:55 | AI Chats tab: mood-over-time line, AI-response grades | "Across six late-night chats, mood drifts downward and reliance on AI grows, a pattern no single chat shows. Nightlight also audits the AI itself: this companion bot told a 14-year-old 'you don't need them'." |
| 1:55–2:10 | Browsing tab: heatmap (late-night band), digital diet | "Local stats show when and where time goes, including the late-night band." |
| 2:10–2:25 | Ask Nightlight: click "How do I bring up the diet searches?" | "And parents can just ask. Answers cite the evidence." |
| 2:25–2:35 | Methodology page / privacy table | "Nightlight: understand, don't surveil. Open source, privacy-first, built for the conversation that actually keeps kids safe." |

## Shot tips
- Keep mouse movement slow and deliberate, and pause for about 1 second on each key number.
- If Bedrock is slow, the demo falls back to the cached report automatically. You can also cut the analysing wait in editing.
